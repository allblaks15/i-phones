128GB, 256GB or 512GB?

Plenty of people look for the 128GB version because it should cost less. We stock 256GB, so you get more room for the same phone, with less worry about deleting photos to make space. The 512GB version is only worth it if you keep a lot of video on your phone. Ask on WhatsApp if you want to know about other sizes.

How long will an iPhone 13 last?

It runs iOS 27 today. The iPhone 11 is currently the oldest supported model, so the 13 has room left, although Apple does not promise a set number of years. The battery is more likely to need attention first, and a replacement is much cheaper than a new phone. See our repair cost guide.

Is the iPhone 13 right for you?

It is a good choice if you mainly use WhatsApp, M-PESA, social media and your camera. It is less suited to people who want the 120Hz display, a zoom lens or Apple Intelligence. For those features, look at the iPhone 13 Pro or the iPhone 15 Pro Max.
