## eSIM or physical SIM?

Each storage size comes in two versions. The eSIM-only version has no physical SIM tray and is usually a little cheaper. The Physical SIM + eSIM version lets you keep using a normal SIM card. If you use a regular Safaricom or Airtel SIM and do not want to switch, choose the physical version. If you are happy to move to eSIM, confirm that your line supports it first.
