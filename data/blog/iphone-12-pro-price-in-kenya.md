## What you get

The [iPhone 12 Pro](product:iphone-12-pro) has a 6.1-inch OLED display, the A14 Bionic chip, LiDAR, three 12MP cameras with 2x zoom, a stainless steel frame, MagSafe and 5G. Its 2,815 mAh battery is modest, so check battery health carefully on any used unit.

## iPhone 12 Pro or iPhone 13?

{compare:iphone-12-pro,iphone-13,iphone-12-pro-max}

The [iPhone 13](post:iphone-13-price-in-kenya) has a newer chip and more battery capacity, which most people will notice more than the zoom. Choose the 12 Pro if you shoot a lot of portraits or want the extra lens. If you want a bigger screen, see the [iPhone 12 Pro Max](post:iphone-12-pro-max-price-in-kenya).

## Who is it for?

The iPhone 12 Pro suits people who want a compact Pro phone with a telephoto lens, at one of our lowest prices. The extra lens and LiDAR help with portraits, including in low light. If you mainly use your phone for WhatsApp, M-PESA and social media, the extra camera may not matter to you.

## Storage options

We stock the 256GB version. Apple also made 128GB and 512GB versions. Since 256GB is above the base size, it is worth comparing carefully with cheaper adverts, which may be for a 128GB phone.

## Things to know

- **Battery:** at 2,815 mAh and with years of possible use behind it, this is the first thing to check.
- **Display:** the 60Hz screen is sharp, but not as smooth as the 120Hz display on the 13 Pro.
- **Charging:** it uses Lightning and supports MagSafe.
- **Software:** it runs iOS 27 but does not support Apple Intelligence.
- **Network:** it supports 5G where available.
