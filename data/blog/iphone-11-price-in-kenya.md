The iPhone 11 is now the oldest iPhone that still gets new iOS versions. We do not list it, but here is a fair picture of what it costs and whether it is still a good buy.

> These are asking prices from other sellers' listings, checked on 2 November 2026. We have not verified them, and stock changes quickly.

## iPhone 11 price in Kenya

- **Ex-UK:** about KSh 27,000 to 32,000 in the listings we found
- **Used, from individual sellers:** often lower, with wider differences in condition
- **New and sealed:** some shops still list these, at far higher prices. Check that "new" really means sealed.

Storage options are 64GB, 128GB and 256GB. Avoid 64GB if you can, because it fills up quickly.

## iPhone 11 Pro and 11 Pro Max price in Kenya

- **iPhone 11 Pro Max, Ex-UK:** about KSh 37,000 to 42,000 in the listings we found
- **iPhone 11 Pro:** we did not find enough reliable listings to give a fair range. Expect it to sit near the 11 Pro Max, and compare storage carefully.

Pro models came in 64GB, 256GB and 512GB. There was no 128GB version, so question any advert offering one.

## What you get

- **iPhone 11:** 6.1-inch LCD, A13 chip, dual 12MP cameras, 3,110 mAh
- **iPhone 11 Pro:** 5.8-inch OLED, A13, triple cameras, 3,046 mAh
- **iPhone 11 Pro Max:** 6.5-inch OLED, A13, triple cameras, 3,969 mAh

All three run iOS 27, but none has 5G or Apple Intelligence.

## Should you buy one?

An iPhone 11 is the cheapest iPhone we would still consider, because it gets current iOS. But at this age, battery wear is likely, so budget for a [battery replacement](post:iphone-screen-and-battery-replacement-cost-in-kenya) and use our [used iPhone checklist](post:refurbished-iphones-in-kenya). If you can stretch your budget, the [iPhone 13](post:iphone-13-price-in-kenya) at {from:iphone-13} adds 5G and a brighter OLED display. There is no [iPhone 11 mini or 11 Plus](post:iphone-models-that-do-not-exist).

## 64GB, 128GB or 256GB?

The iPhone 11 came in 64GB, 128GB and 256GB. If you can, choose at least 128GB, since 64GB leaves little room after iOS, apps and WhatsApp media. When a seller quotes a low price, check the storage first. Many of the cheapest adverts are for 64GB phones.

## Buying an iPhone 11 in Nairobi

Whichever seller you choose, ask for the condition, battery health, storage and warranty in writing, even on WhatsApp. Our guide on [where to buy an iPhone in Kenya](post:where-to-buy-iphone-in-kenya) compares shops, Jumia, Jiji and Safaricom. Prices from older adverts can be misleading, so compare only current listings.
