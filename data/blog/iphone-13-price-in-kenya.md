## How long will an iPhone 13 last?

It runs iOS 27 today. The iPhone 11 is currently the oldest supported model, so the 13 has room left, although Apple does not promise a set number of years. The battery is more likely to need attention first, and a replacement is much cheaper than a new phone. See our [repair cost guide](post:iphone-screen-and-battery-replacement-cost-in-kenya).

## Is the iPhone 13 right for you?

It is a good choice if you mainly use WhatsApp, M-PESA, social media and your camera. It is less suited to people who want the 120Hz display, a zoom lens or Apple Intelligence. For those features, look at the [iPhone 13 Pro](post:iphone-13-pro-price-in-kenya) or the [iPhone 15 Pro Max](post:iphone-15-price-in-kenya).
