## What changes the price of an iPhone in Kenya

Two listings for the "same" iPhone can be tens of thousands of shillings apart. Four things explain most of the gap:

- **Condition.** Brand New phones are sealed. Ex-UK phones are pre-owned imports and cost much less. Read [what Ex-UK really means](post:refurbished-iphones-in-kenya) before you buy one.
- **Storage.** Moving from 256GB to 512GB on an [iPhone 17 Pro Max](product:iphone-17-pro-max) adds tens of thousands of shillings.
- **SIM type.** Physical SIM + eSIM versions usually cost more than eSIM-only versions. Check that your line supports eSIM before choosing one.
- **Colour.** On the iPhone 18 series, some colours cost more. Burgundy is priced above Black on the [iPhone 18 Pro Max](product:iphone-18-pro-max).
