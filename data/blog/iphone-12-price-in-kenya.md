## What you get

Both have an OLED display, the A14 Bionic, dual 12MP cameras and 5G. The iPhone 12 has a 6.1-inch screen and a 2,815 mAh battery. The 12 mini has a 5.4-inch screen and a small 2,227 mAh battery, so battery life is its main weakness.

## iPhone 12 storage: 64GB, 128GB or 256GB?

The standard iPhone 12 and 12 mini came in 64GB, 128GB and 256GB. Avoid 64GB if you can, since iOS, apps and WhatsApp media will fill it quickly. If you see a cheap price, check the storage first, as that is often the reason.

## iPhone 12 or iPhone 12 Pro?

The Pro adds a telephoto lens, LiDAR, a stainless steel frame and more memory. The standard iPhone 12 is lighter and comes in more colours. In daily use, the screen and speed are similar, so the choice comes down to the camera and your budget.

## What to check before buying

These phones are from 2020, so check Battery Health, confirm the seller has signed out of iCloud, and test Face ID. The phone should show its real model name in Settings, under General and About. Since the mini's battery is small, a used one with a worn battery may struggle to last a full day. Read our [used iPhone checklist](post:refurbished-iphones-in-kenya) before you pay.
