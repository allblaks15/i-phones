## What you get

The [iPhone 14 Pro](product:iphone-14-pro) has a 6.1-inch 120Hz Always-On display, the A16 Bionic chip and a 48MP main camera with ultra wide and 3x telephoto lenses. Its 3,200 mAh battery is the main trade-off, as it lasts noticeably less than the Pro Max.

## 14 Pro or 14 Pro Max?

Choose the [14 Pro Max](post:iphone-14-pro-max-price-in-kenya) if battery life and screen size matter most. Choose the 14 Pro if you prefer a smaller phone and want to save the difference.

## 14 Pro or 13 Pro?

The [iPhone 13 Pro](post:iphone-13-pro-price-in-kenya) at {from:iphone-13-pro} also has a 120Hz display and three cameras. The 14 Pro adds the Dynamic Island, Always-On display, a 48MP camera, Crash Detection and a faster chip. If you take many photos, the extra cost is easy to justify.

## Who should choose the iPhone 14 Pro?

It suits people who care about photos but prefer a smaller phone. The 48MP camera and 3x zoom are a real step up from a standard iPhone, and the phone is easy to use with one hand. It is less suited to heavy users who are away from a charger all day. In that case the Pro Max's larger battery is worth considering.

## Storage options

We stock the 256GB version. Apple also sold 128GB, 512GB and 1TB versions, which you may see at other sellers at different prices. Compare the same storage size whenever you compare prices. If you record a lot of 4K video, avoid 128GB.

## Battery and charging

The 14 Pro charges through Lightning and also supports MagSafe wireless charging. You will need a USB-C to Lightning cable for fast charging. On an Ex-UK phone, check Battery Health in Settings when you visit, since this phone's smaller battery leaves less margin once it has aged.
