Ex-UK, refurbished, used, second hand: Kenyan sellers use these words loosely. They are not the same thing, and the difference can be worth thousands of shillings.

## What the words usually mean

- **Brand New:** sealed in the original box and never used.
- **Ex-UK:** a pre-owned phone imported from the UK and resold here. The condition varies from phone to phone.
- **Refurbished:** a used phone that has been checked or repaired. Ask who did the work and which parts were changed.
- **Second hand or used:** bought from a previous owner, often on a marketplace, with little or no checking.
- **Apple Certified Refurbished:** restored by Apple itself. Do not assume a phone in Kenya is this unless the seller can prove it.

## Your checklist before paying

Run through this on any used iPhone, including ours:

- **Battery:** open Settings, then Battery, then Battery Health.
- **Parts and service history:** in Settings, General, About, newer iPhones show whether the screen, battery or camera has been replaced.
- **Activation lock:** the seller must sign out of iCloud and erase the phone in front of you.
- **IMEI:** dial *#06# and check that it matches Settings and the box, if there is one.
- **Face ID or Touch ID, cameras, speakers and charging port:** test each one, then make a call and test mobile data with your own SIM.
- **Water damage or dents:** look closely at the frame and around the camera.
