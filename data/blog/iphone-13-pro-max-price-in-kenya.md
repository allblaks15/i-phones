## Why people still choose it

- 6.7-inch 120Hz ProMotion display
- A15 Bionic with a 5-core GPU
- Triple 12MP cameras with 3x telephoto
- 4,352 mAh battery, one of the largest of its generation

## What you give up

Compared with the [iPhone 14 Pro Max](post:iphone-14-pro-max-price-in-kenya), you miss the Dynamic Island, the Always-On display and the 48MP main camera. It also uses Lightning rather than USB-C and has no Apple Intelligence.

## Which storage should you pick?

We stock 256GB, which suits most buyers. People also search for the 128GB, 512GB and 1TB versions. The 128GB can feel tight if you film in 4K, since ProRes and high-quality video use a lot of space. The 512GB and 1TB versions make sense only if you store large videos on the phone. Ask on WhatsApp if you want a size we do not list.

## Who is it good for?

The 13 Pro Max suits people who want a large screen and long battery life on a moderate budget. It is great for video, reading and long days away from a charger. It is heavy, at 240g, so try it in your hand at the shop if weight matters to you.

## How long will it stay supported?

It runs iOS 27 today. Apple has not said how long that will continue, but it is newer than the iPhone 11, which is currently the oldest supported model. That suggests a few more years of support, though nothing is guaranteed.
