## What you get

The [iPhone 12 Pro Max](product:iphone-12-pro-max) has a 6.7-inch OLED display, the A14 Bionic chip, a stainless steel frame, LiDAR and three 12MP cameras with 2.5x zoom. Its 3,687 mAh battery is smaller than the 13 Pro Max's 4,352 mAh.

## Is it still worth it in 2026?

It is a sensible choice if you want a big display at a modest price. It still runs iOS 27 and supports 5G. The compromises are a 60Hz display, Lightning charging and no Apple Intelligence.

## 128GB, 256GB or 512GB?

We stock 256GB, which gives you more room than the base 128GB model. Apple also made a 512GB version. If you are comparing an advert for this phone, ask which size it is, because a cheaper price often means less storage.

## Who is it for?

It suits people who want a large display for video, reading and browsing, and who do not want to spend much more. It also has the best camera of the iPhone 12 range, with a larger main sensor and sensor-shift stabilisation. It is heavy at 226g, so it is worth holding one first.

## What to check on a phone this age

Released in 2020, this phone may well have a worn battery or replaced parts. Check Battery Health, then open Settings, General, About to see whether any parts have been changed. Test the Face ID, all three cameras and the speakers before you pay. Screen repairs on large iPhones can be expensive, so look closely for cracks and marks.
