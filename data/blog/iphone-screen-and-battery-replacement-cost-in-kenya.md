A cracked screen or a tired battery does not always mean a new phone. We do not advertise a repair service ourselves, but it helps to know the likely cost before you decide between a repair and an upgrade.

> These are advertised prices from Nairobi repair shop websites, checked on 2 November 2026. They vary widely and some may be out of date, so get a written quote.

## iPhone battery replacement prices

- **iPhone 11 to 13:** roughly KSh 2,800 to 4,500 in the listings we found
- **iPhone 14 and 15:** some shops list up to about KSh 9,500 to 11,000
- **iPhone 17 series:** listings ranged from about KSh 6,000 to 9,500

We did not find reliable current prices for the iPhone 5, 6, 7, 8 or X. These are older phones, so check first whether the repair is worth it at all. Our [older iPhone guides](post:old-iphones-in-kenya) can help you decide.

## iPhone screen replacement prices

- **iPhone 11 and 12:** from about KSh 6,500 to 8,000
- **iPhone 13 and 14:** from about KSh 8,500 to 22,000
- **iPhone 15 and newer:** from about KSh 15,500 to more than KSh 50,000

Why such a wide range? The part. An original Apple screen and a cheap aftermarket one can look alike but differ in brightness, colour and touch response.

## Questions to ask before any repair

- Is the part original, a pull from another iPhone, or aftermarket?
- Will the phone show an "unknown part" message afterwards?
- Will Face ID and True Tone still work?
- How long is the warranty on the repair?
- Is the quoted price final?

## Repair or upgrade?

Compare the repair quote with the price of a better phone. If a screen costs a large share of what the phone is worth, or your phone no longer gets iOS updates, upgrading can be smarter.
