## What you get

The [iPhone 13 Pro](product:iphone-13-pro) has the A15 Bionic with a 5-core GPU, a 6.1-inch ProMotion display, three 12MP cameras with 3x zoom and a 3,095 mAh battery. It has a stainless steel frame and uses Lightning for charging.

## Who should buy the iPhone 13 Pro?

It suits buyers who want a smooth 120Hz display and a zoom lens without a large phone. Portrait and macro photos are a real strength. If you do not need zoom, the standard iPhone 13 saves you money. If you use your phone heavily all day, the bigger battery in the [13 Pro Max](post:iphone-13-pro-max-price-in-kenya) is worth paying for.

## Storage options

We stock 256GB. Apple also made 128GB, 512GB and 1TB versions, so a price you see elsewhere may be for a different size. Before comparing, ask for the exact storage, condition and battery health. Prices for the same model can differ a lot once these are taken into account.

## Network and charging

The 13 Pro supports 5G where your network offers it, and works on a physical SIM plus eSIM on many versions. Ask which SIM setup the specific phone has. It charges through Lightning and also supports MagSafe, so check our [charger guide](post:iphone-charger-and-accessories-in-kenya) if you need a new cable.

## How long will it last?

It runs iOS 27 today, and the A15 chip handles everyday apps comfortably. Battery health is usually what shows its age first, so weigh that in when you compare phones.
