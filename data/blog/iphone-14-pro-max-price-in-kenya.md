## 128GB, 256GB, 512GB or 1TB?

Many people search for specific sizes of this phone. Here is a simple way to think about it:

- **128GB:** fine for light users, but 4K video and WhatsApp media fill it quickly. We do not stock this size regularly.
- **256GB:** the sensible choice for most buyers, and what we stock.
- **512GB:** for people who keep large video libraries on the phone.
- **1TB:** mainly for creators who film a lot and do not want to use cloud storage.

Expect prices to rise with each step, and ask on WhatsApp if you need a size other than 256GB.

## Who should buy it?

The 14 Pro Max suits people who want a big, bright display and all-day battery life, and who take many photos. If you mostly use WhatsApp, social media and M-PESA, you may not need it, and an [iPhone 13](post:iphone-13-price-in-kenya) will save you money.

## Checking eSIM on this phone

Ours is listed as eSIM. Before you pay, ask your network whether it can move your line to eSIM, and have that sorted before you need the phone. You will also want to keep your M-PESA line working, so confirm the steps with your provider.
